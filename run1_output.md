**Why this works for weight loss:** every meal pairs a measured grain portion
with dal, sundal, curd or sprouts for protein, which keeps hunger down on
ordinary portions. Fermented breakfasts (idli, dosa, adai) are filling without
deep frying, and snacks are protein- or fibre-based instead of packaged
fried snacks.
